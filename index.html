DoNow

Privacy policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

DoNow helps you focus by blocking the distracting apps you choose during a focus session. This policy explains the information the Android app accesses, why it uses it, and what stays on your device.

This policy describes the current version of DoNow, before subscriptions or billing are implemented.

1. Information on your device

DoNow accesses and stores information locally to set up app blocking and run your focus sessions:

Your app choices and blocking consent
The package identifiers of the apps you select, and whether you have agreed to blocking. A package identifier is Android’s technical name for an app.
Onboarding progress and goals
Your setup progress, whether you have completed onboarding, and your chosen focus goal.
Tasks and drafts
Your task category, any custom task text you enter, and draft task and duration choices. Custom text may contain personal information if you include it.
Session details
The session duration, start and end times, and completion status. DoNow also stores elapsed-clock readings and the device’s reboot count to restore the timer accurately. The reboot count is not a unique device identifier.
Apps available to select
DoNow reads the names and package identifiers of installed launcher apps to display the app picker. The available-app list is held in memory; your selected package identifiers are saved locally.

2. Accessibility and app blocking

With Android Accessibility access enabled, DoNow’s Accessibility Service receives window-state-change events and reads the originating app’s package identifier. This helps it notice when you open an app.

These events may be received while the service is enabled, even outside focus sessions and for apps you have not selected. DoNow checks the identifier against your saved choices. Blocking requires all three conditions:

  • You have given blocking consent in DoNow.
  • The app is in your selected-app list.
  • A focus session is active and has not expired.

When these conditions are met, DoNow shows its blocking screen with your task and remaining time. It temporarily holds the observed app identifier in memory to handle these checks. It does not save an app-use history.

The service does not retrieve window content, read typed text or messages, capture screenshots, or simulate taps.

3. Transmission and third parties

The current app has no Internet permission or developer-operated data-upload path. It processes the information described here on your device; it does not send that information to the publisher or to a developer-operated server.

No mechanism for sharing or selling this information is implemented in the app. The inspected release dependencies contain no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, networking, or billing SDK.

DoNow does use third-party software libraries, including AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, and Kotlin libraries, to provide its interface and local functionality. This policy’s description of DoNow does not describe separate data practices of Android, Google Play, your device manufacturer, or other apps and services you use.

Subscriptions and payment processing are not part of the current app. This policy does not describe a future billing implementation.

4. Storage and retention

DoNow stores its local files and preferences in Android’s private app storage, without additional app-level encryption. Draft choices may also be kept in Android’s saved screen state.

Your selected apps, consent, and onboarding choices remain available for later use. DoNow keeps a current session record rather than a session-history database. When the timer expires, the completed session record may remain. Successfully ending a session clears its current durable record, but draft task text may remain.

Older preferences retained during a storage migration may also remain on the device, although the app no longer uses them once the replacement state files exist. Ending a session or changing a selection does not guarantee deletion of every older local copy.

If saving a change fails, the previously saved session or blocking settings may remain in effect. DoNow reports storage errors rather than treating a failed save as successful.

5. Your controls and data removal

Change the apps you select

You can select or deselect apps in DoNow’s app-selection screen. Successfully saved changes update which apps are eligible for blocking.

Turn off blocking inside DoNow

The “Turn off blocking” control withdraws your blocking consent when the change is saved. It does not itself disable the Android Accessibility Service. App-identifying events may still be received while Android Accessibility access remains enabled.

Disable Android Accessibility access

To stop the service from receiving accessibility events, disable DoNow’s Accessibility Service in your device’s Android Accessibility settings. Menu names can vary by device. Disabling this access stops accessibility-based blocking; it does not erase your saved selections or end or delete the focus session.

Remove locally stored information

DoNow does not currently have an in-app “delete all data” feature. Clearing DoNow’s app storage through Android settings or uninstalling the app is the expected way to remove its locally stored information. Clearing storage is different from clearing only the cache.

These actions rely on Android’s storage handling. They are not a guarantee of secure erasure or deletion from every location, including copies made outside DoNow.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy will need to be updated if DoNow’s information handling changes, including when subscriptions or other services are introduced. Refer to the effective date at the top to identify this version.
